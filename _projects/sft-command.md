Built a 200+ backend-function platform with live Plaid (account linking, balance sync), Stripe (payments, subscriptions, identity verification), and Yahoo Finance integrations — 50+ database schemas, multi-tenant isolation, RBAC, compliance audit trails.

Architected five domain-scoped AI agents coordinated into a single compiled answer, plus predictive analytics (5-year net worth projection, cash flow forecasting, Monte Carlo simulation).

Eight months of direct outreach to fiduciary buyers produced zero pilots — a distribution and trust-network problem, not a technical one. Made the call to stop pursuing that product rather than keep funding a sales motion I'm not suited for; code retained as capability evidence.

**The entity continued.** SFT Command LLC wasn't dissolved — it was renamed and became [NARVI LLC](/about/), the parent company behind A.R.C.H.O.N. and [Anubis](/projects/private-rd/).
